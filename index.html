EasyOps

EasyOps is a workforce management platform for South African businesses. It helps employers track attendance and check-ins, manage shifts and rosters, handle leave, export payroll data, and stay compliant with the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA).

Users can sign in to EasyOps with email, phone or Google. When you choose Google sign-in, EasyOps receives only your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them solely to identify you and give you access to your organisation's workspace.
