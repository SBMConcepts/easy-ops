## 1. Privacy Policy

### (POPIA & PAIA Compliant Privacy Notice)

This Privacy Policy explains how EasyOps collects, processes, stores, and protects Personal Information in its capacity as a B2B SaaS platform under the **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)**.

> ### IMPORTANT POPIA CONTEXTUAL DISTINCTION
>
> 1. **CUSTOMER** (Juristic/Natural Person purchasing subscriptions) = **RESPONSIBLE PARTY**
> 2. **WORKFORCE** (Employees (“Members”)/Contractors of Customer registering on platform) = **DATA SUBJECTS**
> 3. **EASYOPS** ((Pty) Ltd EasyOps) = **OPERATOR** (Data Processor)
>
> _As an Operator, EasyOps processes all workforce records solely on the written instruction of the Customer and maintains absolute data sovereignty within South African borders._

### 1.1 Types of Information Collected and Processed

To deliver workforce management, attendance tracking, and payroll support, EasyOps processes:

- **Customer Registration Information:** Legal entity details, registration numbers, tax/VAT details, physical addresses, billing contact names, and emails.
- **Workforce Personal Information (on behalf of Customer):** Full names, South African identity numbers or passport numbers, contact numbers, email addresses, employee numbers, job descriptions, and leave records.
- **Workforce Special Personal Information & Biometrics:** GPS location check-in data, facial images, or verification vectors (where biometric check-in is enabled by Customer), and basic health check logs.
- **Juristic Person Protection:** Unlike the GDPR, POPIA protects the data rights of legal entities. EasyOps extends all security and data processing protections to our B2B customers.

### 1.2 Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

Processing is performed under the instruction of the Customer for the following lawful bases:

- **Contractual Necessity:** To execute the workforce tracking and platform operational agreements with the Customer.
- **Legal Obligations:** To support the Customer in meeting statutory retention obligations under the _Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA)_, _Labour Relations Act (LRA)_, and SARS requirements.
- **Consent:** Where biometric check-in or sensitive tracking is requested, the Customer warrants they have collected appropriate, un-coerced written consent from their employees.

### 1.3 Data Sovereignty and Storage Locations

- **South African Infrastructure:** In line with our South Africa-first policy, all primary hosting environments, database instances, and backup servers are maintained within physical data centres located inside the borders of the Republic of South Africa.
- **Cross-Border Policy:** EasyOps does not transfer any customer or workforce personal information outside the Republic of South Africa unless contractually requested by the Customer and legally approved in writing by the EasyOps board of directors, subject to Section 72 of POPIA.

### 1.4 Security Safeguards (Section 19 POPIA compliance)

EasyOps establishes and maintains robust technical and organisational security measures:

- **Access Controls & Isolation:** Multi-tenant logical isolation mechanisms. No client tenant can access, view, or intercept the data storage tables of another tenant.
- **Data Security:** Encryption of data at rest and in transit using advanced transport layer security.
- **Device Management:** Restrictive administrative privilege protocols for EasyOps engineering teams.
- **System Vulnerability Protection:** Continuous automated security logs, password hashing, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforcement.

### 1.5 Security Breach Notification (Section 22 POPIA compliance)

Where there are reasonable grounds to believe that Personal Information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person:

- EasyOps will notify the impacted Customer (Responsible Party) in writing within **24 to 72 hours**.
- The notification will detail the nature of the breach, suspected volumes, affected parameters, mitigation measures taken, and the contact details of the Information Officer.
- The Customer, as the Responsible Party, bears the legal obligation under POPIA to notify the South African Information Regulator and the affected workforce data subjects. EasyOps will assist with technical telemetry.

### 1.6 Data Retention, Deletion, and Return

- EasyOps retains personal information only to support platform delivery or as directed by the Customer.
- Upon termination of a Customer subscription, EasyOps offers a **90-day retention window** to allow the Customer to export historical attendance and payroll support data.
- Following this 90-day window, or on the Customer's express instruction, all records are permanently purged or anonymised using secure destruction methodologies, subject to any contrary statutory data-keeping laws.

### 1.7 Information Officer Contact Details

For any privacy queries, access requests (PAIA), or corrections under Section 24 of POPIA, contact:

- **EasyOps Information Officer**
- **Email:** <admin@easyops.co.za>
